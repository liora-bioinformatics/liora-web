Features

Powerful where it counts, simple where it matters.

Vantage is designed to be understood in minutes and relied on for years. Here's a closer look at what it does and how it fits into your day.

Unified workspace

Bring people, data, and tasks into one organized place with a layout your team actually enjoys using.

Automation

Set rules once and let routine work run itself — reminders, hand-offs, and reports without the manual effort.

Analytics & reporting

Turn raw activity into clear metrics with dashboards, exports, and scheduled summaries for stakeholders.

Roles & permissions

Give every person exactly the access they need with granular, easy-to-manage permission controls.

Integrations & API

Connect the tools you already use, or build your own workflows on top of a clean, documented API.

Security & compliance

Encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, and access controls that stand up to scrutiny.
