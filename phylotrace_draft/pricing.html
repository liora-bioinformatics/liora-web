Subscription FAQ

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and changes take effect on your next billing cycle. Your data stays intact throughout.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You'll have full access to explore before deciding.

How does billing work?

Subscriptions are billed monthly or annually per user. Annual billing includes a discount. You can cancel anytime, no long-term contract required on standard plans.

What's included in support?

All plans include support from our team. Professional and Enterprise plans add priority response times and dedicated contacts.

Do you offer discounts?

We offer annual billing discounts and special pricing for larger teams. Contact us to discuss what works for your organization.
