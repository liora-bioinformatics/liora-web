Deployment & onboarding

Up and running the way that suits you.

Whether you prefer a fully managed cloud service or a deployment inside your own environment, we make setup smooth and predictable — with people alongside you the whole way.

Deployment options

Choose where Vantage runs.

Managed cloud

We host, secure, and maintain everything. The fastest path to value with zero infrastructure to manage on your side.

  • Instant provisioning
  • Automatic updates & backups
  • Monitored 24/7

Private cloud

Deploy into your own cloud account for greater control over data residency and networking, with our team's guidance.

  • Your cloud, your control
  • Data residency options
  • Guided configuration

On-premises

Run Vantage entirely within your own datacenter to meet strict security or regulatory requirements.

  • Full data isolation
  • Meets strict compliance needs
  • Deployment support included

Not sure which fits? Our team will help you choose based on your security, scale, and compliance needs.
